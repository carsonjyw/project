# ELSEWHERE

**Get lost. Find something.**

## Project Idea

ELSEWHERE is an AI-assisted city discovery experience that helps students and young adults explore New York City.

Instead of spending time switching between Instagram, Google Maps, screenshots, and saved recommendations, users can turn their interests and available time into personalized, realistic city adventures.

## The Problem

Discovering interesting places in NYC is easy. Actually visiting them is harder.

Recommendations are scattered across different platforms, saved places are often forgotten, and planning an outing requires figuring out which locations are worth visiting, how far apart they are, and how much time is available.

**ELSEWHERE aims to turn scattered inspiration into real experiences.**

## Target Users

Our primary users are students and young adults living in or visiting New York City who:

- Enjoy exploring new neighborhoods, cafés, restaurants, bookstores, and hidden gems.
- Regularly save recommendations from social media, maps, and friends.
- Have limited time or budgets for exploring.
- Want spontaneous adventures without spending too much time planning.

## Initial Vision

Users will be able to describe the kind of outing they want, such as:

*"I have three hours this Saturday. I want to explore a new neighborhood, visit a bookstore, and find a good coffee shop."*

ELSEWHERE will help them discover real places, organize a feasible route, and save their discoveries in a personal NYC Atlas.

## Project Direction

**Primary focus: Design**

I want ELSEWHERE to feel less like a conventional travel planner and more like an interactive city guide—combining editorial design, a modern atlas, and playful but purposeful interactions.

The goal is to make exploring the city feel exciting while keeping the experience practical and easy to use.
